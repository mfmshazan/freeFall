Spacecraft fire safety research

Fire safety beyond Earth rests on three questions: how do we keep a fire from starting, how do we notice it early, and how do we put it out. These studies describe the materials, combustion conditions and apparatus that were tested.

Prevent

Explore material choices and identify the conditions — oxygen, pressure, airflow — that still need dedicated fire testing before they can be trusted.

Detect

Understand smoke transport in microgravity, sensor placement, ventilation, and how lunar dust can interfere with detection.

Respond

Review how atmosphere, flow and fuel influenced extinction in the original experiments.
