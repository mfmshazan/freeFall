04 / About FlameAtlas

Exploring fire where gravity changes.

FlameAtlas turns NASA's microgravity combustion experiments into a visual experience for students, educators and researchers. Every experiment links back to its source, values are tagged as reported or unknown, and illustrative animations are always labelled as such.
