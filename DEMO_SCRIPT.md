# Flame in Freefall — two-minute demo

### 0:00 · Orbit and atmosphere

Open **Flame Lab** in free-fall Earth orbit. Choose **Acrylic rod** and leave airflow at zero.
Point out the circular still-air model and L/D of 1. Change oxygen or absolute pressure and
show the separate oxygen percentage and partial-pressure readings. The model values and
heuristic spread are exploratory.

### 0:25 · Palette and chemistry

Raise oxygen to 25% or more and show the model’s PMMA palette label. Open **Evidence**, select
**Soot and optical appearance**, then choose **CFI-G evidence**. The final report identified
a cool phase in run 21174C3 using radiometry and intensified-camera imagery. The evidence
card gives the burner feed and chamber atmosphere separately.

### 0:50 · Recorded ventilation and supported habitats

In **Research stories**, follow **Open Saffire-I replay** from **Orbital ventilation**.
Scrub the imagery and sensor traces and show the report's concurrent and opposed rates.
The opposed pass reused the sheet remnant. BASS-II shows a separate comparison of two
specimens with different gas histories and before/after readings.

Return to **Evidence**, choose **Supported habitats** and show the separate LUCI flame-base
and flame-tip fits. The experiment used rocket rotation to simulate lunar acceleration.
Choose Mars in the Flame Lab and follow its scenario link to the reviewed evidence.

### 1:30 · Detection and suppression research

Choose **Smoke detection** and show the SAME source on particle transport and aging.
Open **FLEX evidence** from **Suppression research** and show the original mixture and
droplet-specific units. The diameter-squared rate uses mm²/s.

### 1:55 · Close

Open **Validation status**. The Phase 4 audit found zero eligible independent Saffire runs.
Both the API and offline mode use the deterministic physics baseline. Follow the source
link to Table I and open the report for the exclusions and missing measurements.

## Before the demo

Start `npm run dev` in `frontend/`. To show API mode, start `uvicorn main:app --port 8000`
in the backend environment. Check both replays and the model source badge. Original reports
require internet access.
