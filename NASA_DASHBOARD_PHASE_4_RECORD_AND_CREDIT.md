Implements "54–60 Record and credit" and the "30-second walkthrough" in [NASA_DASHBOARD_60_MINUTE_PLAN.md](NASA_DASHBOARD_60_MINUTE_PLAN.md). Requires [Phase 3](NASA_DASHBOARD_PHASE_3_INTEGRATION.md).

1. Record: 0–6 s Moon example with the high-oxygen cabin; 6–15 s ranked record, condition mismatch, **Explain NASA evidence**, source and limit; 15–23 s Detection in Fix It; 23–30 s documented lunar sensor transfer in the Earth card. Cut provider wait time from the recording if needed.
2. Credit in the submission: the NASA dataset and report actually used (check the challenge Resources tab), how they power ranking and the briefing, and the AI provider/model and its role.
3. Use the team's demo URL, or record locally through Vite and FastAPI. If provider access never worked, describe live AI as pending.
4. Leave out spinoff variants, live PSI ingestion, chat and retraining.
